Benji Pays connects QuickBooks Online, QuickBooks Desktop, Xero, and Business Central to Benji Payments and other gateways so invoice payments post to your books automatically. Not a general ledger. Benji Pays is invoice-to-cash software that sits between your accounting system and your payment rails, automating collections for managed service providers.

**Areas:** Payments · Accounting · Integration · QuickBooks · Xero
